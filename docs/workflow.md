# EcomInsight AI — Project Workflow

## Overview

EcomInsight AI is an AI-powered e-commerce analytics platform that transforms raw sales data into interactive visual insights, forecasts, and business recommendations.

The following workflow demonstrates the major modules of the completed application.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides an executive overview of e-commerce performance through key performance indicators and interactive filters.

![Dashboard Overview](screenshots/01-dashboard-overview.png)

---

## 2. Sales Trends

The Sales Trends module visualizes monthly sales and profit performance to identify trends and changes over time.

![Sales Trends](screenshots/02-sales-trends.png)

---

## 3. Category Analysis

The Category Analysis module compares product categories and sub-categories based on sales, profit, quantity, and overall performance.

![Category Analysis](screenshots/03-category-analysis.png)

---

## 4. Geographical Analysis

The Geographical Analysis module provides state-wise sales and profit insights to understand regional performance.

![Geographical Analysis](screenshots/04-geographical-analysis.png)

---

## 5. Payment Analysis

The Payment Analysis module examines sales distribution across different payment methods.

![Payment Analysis](screenshots/05-payment-analysis.png)

---

## 6. Sales Forecasting

The Forecasting module uses historical sales data and Linear Regression to estimate future sales trends.

![Sales Forecasting](screenshots/06-forecasting.png)

---

## 7. AI Business Analyst

The AI Business Analyst provides an interactive interface for exploring business questions and generating data-driven analytical responses.

![AI Business Analyst](screenshots/07-ai-business-analyst.png)

---

## 8. Insights & Recommendations

The Insights & Recommendations module converts analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

![Insights and Recommendations](screenshots/08-insights-recommendations.png)

---
